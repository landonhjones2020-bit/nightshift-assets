## Decisions
- **Lighting uses Roblox's unified lighting**, not `Technology`: the place carries `RBX_LightingTechnologyUnifiedMigration`, so the old "set Technology = Future" step no longer applies. Set `Lighting.LightingStyle = Realistic` and `PrioritizeLightingQuality = true` (scriptable in edit mode). With real assets the first pass was blown out (cream walls + stainless + overlapping lamps + Neon diffusers), so lamp brightness dropped ~40% (kitchen 0.5, counter 0.7, dining pendants 0.5, neon washes 0.9) and the Shift grade moved to exposure −0.25, bloom 0.4 / threshold 2.0. Verified (playtest screenshots): kitchen readable, dining moody with neon spill, office dim (desk lamp).
- **In-world boards are client SurfaceGuis on the boards' Blender role parts** (`Controllers.ShopWorld`; Lobby bay names in `LobbyWorld`): menu boards list the Menu with arrival nights, rules sheets add each night's rules in red, the office photo board shows each Regular's two notes and usual order from `Config.Entities.Regulars` (portrait icons `Portrait_<id>` come with G2), the Break Room board lists the perks bought this run.
- **Player poses come from Blender (batch H) as an overlay** on Roblox's own walk/idle: the R15 thigh is deeper than the LowerTorso is tall, so the deep poses (Crouch, HidePeek, Downed) fold the legs into the hips by 0.16–0.56 studs — unavoidable on block R15 and hidden by clothing on most avatars. HidePeek is a seated tuck (no squat that deep keeps the shins above the floor).
- **A carried crate shows in the hands:** a local Kitchen_Crate in the type's pile colours, held at the clip's `rightHandToPivot` every frame; the spatula and flashlight cosmetics hide while both hands are on it.
- **The Downed card shows who caught you:** the catcher's Blender model, red-lit, fills the card's left side (the ViewportFrame was built but never filled).
- **The Patron finale is a sequence, not a cut to Results** (Bible §7.8): the run is decided the moment the last chunk lands (`PatronFed` blocks catches and the 6:00 AM failure), then HandGrabLift (clients carry the chunk tower from the clip's `grab` event to `chomp`), Chew 1.6 s, Burp (camera shake, dining lamps swing, menu boards rattle), a 3 s sunrise that also lifts a blackout, and only then `win()`. Clients watch it letterboxed from two dining-room shots (cut at `chomp`), HUD hidden, customers and other creatures faded out of the shot. Timings and shots live in `Config.Entities.Patron.finale`.
- **Menu boards rattle instead of spinning** on the burp: they hang flat on the wall, so a full spin would swing through the plaster and the ceiling. They shake in the wall's plane and settle a few degrees crooked.
- **Victory card before the Results panel:** on a win the EMPLOYEE OF THE NIGHT card plays first (3.2 s) and the panel opens after it. A promotion earned at run end becomes a "Promotion" row in Results instead of a banner over the panel. Banners now queue instead of replacing each other.
- **The Results offer docks inside the Results panel** (OfferSlot, beside Discoveries) so the non-blocking card never covers Next shift / Back to HQ. On phones it becomes one strip across the Discoveries row (icon, title, price, close at the 44 px touch minimum) and the Discoveries recap steps aside while it shows. Verified (playtest, Device Simulator Galaxy A06, and 1091 × 753 desktop). Elsewhere the floating Offer card is unchanged.
- **The Patron's Plate has a prompt** (`Give chunk`, Kind PatronPlate) that opens the same serve picker as customers. Before this, nothing on the client sent DeliverChunk, so the finale couldn't be won.
- **Delivered chunk tickets leave the rail**, like served orders. Chunk tickets are titled "Chunk n of N".
- **`/cosmetic <id|all>` cheat (Studio only):** grants a cosmetic (and equips it when one id is given) so each Blender cosmetic can be checked on an avatar without waiting for the Daily Deals rotation. Same guards as every cheat: Studio, place owner, refused in Manager's Keys servers.
- **Cosmetics fit the wearer:** CosmeticService places each cosmetic's pivot on its avatar attachment (BodyFrontAttachment, HatAttachment, Left/RightGripAttachment) and scales aprons and hats by the wearer's UpperTorso/Head width against the `ref_size` the model was fitted to.
- **Rigs that move by script hold only their root.** Seating, the Paperface vault, the hanging Shiver and the window-bound Patron anchor the HumanoidRootPart and leave the bone parts unanchored (an anchored bone part ignores its Motor6D, so the clip would freeze). `CustomerService:holdRoot` / `slideTo` do the sitting and the square-up turn; the importer adds an invisible carrier part for bones with no geometry (the Shiver's and Patron's `Root`).
- **Imported rigs stand on the floor under their spawn marker** (`spawnNPC` raycasts down): markers sit 3 studs up where a placeholder's root centre went, and the real rigs pivot at their feet.
- **Clips carry their authored ground speed** (`ClipSpeedRef_<clip>` from meta.clipSpeedRef); BlenderAnimator scales any moving clip by it, including explicit states like ChaseRun, and falls back to `IdleClip`, then "Idle", then the first "*Idle*" clip (IdleSway, HangIdle). One-shots honour AnimSpeed.
- **Clip-only props and small looks are client-side** (`EntityVisuals`): receipts show only inside their clip window, timed from the rig's `Meta_events` (Mr. Usual's slide, Paperface's place, the Patron's slam); the torn bag corner hides at TearBag's `hideCorner`; humans blink (eyelids export open); Walt's cane hides while `Seated`; Lights-Out's bulb lights #FFE9A8 when `Lit`; a `LookAt` attribute turns the head (eased, ±85°).
- **Mr. Usual sits in booth 4, aisle seat**, and stares at the counter over his shoulder: the booth benches face along the windows, so the brief's "facing the counter" is done with the head turn instead of a sideways seat. Customers who eat use any free booth seat the same way (aisle point → slide in → Sit → Eat → slide out); each booth Seat carries `Aisle` and `Floor` attributes from the map builder.
- **Paperface squares up to the counter before vaulting** (it faced the register diagonally, which sent the vault along the counter) and faces straight across for PlaceReceipt (the clip assumes the counter's near edge 1.1 studs ahead).
- **Lights-Out dissolves with its clip:** power back → Dissolve one-shot (shriek at 0.2 s, sinks through the floor), the client fade starts 1.4 s before `gone`, and it despawns at `gone` (2.07 s); catches stop the moment it starts.
- **The Patron stands 3.5 studs farther out than its meta suggests** (z = −38.1): its hands land on solid wall between the windows here, and at the authored distance the fingers came through the plaster. Its eyes still fill the two centre windows; HandSlam still reaches in.
- **Copy tells on the real models:** the cap turns about the hat socket's vertical axis, staring eyes also scale the eye parts 1.3×, and Mina's name badge is a SurfaceGui on the badge plate (the misspelled tell rewrites it). The Copy eats standing (EatStand, requested from D) — the seated Eat clip folded its legs at the counter.
- **Hats keep compatible hair** (each hat's `fitsHair` meta), else another of the archetype's styles that fits, else none. The janitor's "bald" option is simply no hair asset.
- **Audio comes only from Roblox's licensed library.** The licensed uploads are under creators **ProSoundEffects** (“Courtesy of Pro Sound Effects”) and **APMOfficial** (“Courtesy of APM Music”), not an account named “Roblox”. I treated those two as the licensed library and nothing uploaded by ordinary users was used. Weak matches to replace or trim after listening: toaster_hum (a transformer hum), toaster_ding (6.8 s kitchen timer), counter_bell (4.5 s ring-out), ingredient_squish and paperface_rustle (several hits), earpiece_static and freezer_breathing (not seamless loops), wrap_crinkle (a map rustle), and the four music tracks lean early-60s and may contain vocals.
- **The rampaging Shiver walks the CeilingPath graph** (shortest hop route over the links, following each node's height — it dips under the freezer door header) instead of heading straight for the nearest node through walls, and at the end of its route it creeps up to 6 studs off the node toward its target, so standing between nodes is no longer a safe spot.
- **Player poses are an overlay on Roblox's own animations** (`Controllers.PlayerPoses` + BlenderAnimator overlay mode). Avatars here use AnimationConstraints (Roblox's avatar joint upgrade), not Motor6Ds; both expose `.Transform`, which BlenderAnimator now drives. A player's pose comes from replicated attributes (Downed → HidePeek → Crouch/CrouchWalk → CarryCrate → FlashlightAim); only the joints a clip keys are touched, blended in and out over the Animator's pose each frame; Root translations scale by the avatar's hip height. The whack swing plays locally on every press and for other players when the server's Whack effect names the whacker. Idle until batch H's `AnimData.PlayerR15` is imported.
- **Shop icons:** gift SKUs show what they give (DP_GIFT_VIP → the VIP pass art, DP_GIFT_COINS_2 → that pack); coin and token packs use their own pile art when it exists, else the currency icon.
- **Menu boards show each sandwich's G1 icon** beside its name (faded until the night it joins the menu).
- **nslib exports parts in a canonical order** (triangles sorted, vertices numbered by first use): bpy's UV sphere listed its faces differently on every run, so unchanged meshes got new hashes and were republished on every import.
- Dev command **`/provoke <entity>`** (Studio only) triggers an entity's turn — Paperface vault, Mr. Usual stalk, Shiver rampage, Lights-Out dissolve — so they can be checked without waiting out a ticket.
- **Clock-in memo on Night 1 shows only the voicemail** (the rules sheet beside it already lists tonight's rules; showing both duplicated and clipped the memo).
- **Compact layouts are twins, not reflowed originals** (Bible §13.9): a cluster `X` gets a sibling `XCompact`; both carry `Layout = Regular/Compact` and UIController shows the one for the current viewport (< 560 px tall = compact). Controllers wire both and fill the one that is showing (`UI:variants`, `UI:setShown`), so rotating or resizing never loses state. Sizes are set from the smallest target: on a Galaxy A06 the safe area is 705 × 280 px, so 1 reference px ≈ 0.26 px and a 44 px target needs 170 reference px.
- **Cook View on phones:** trays + toaster lanes left (the lanes panel is the Collect button — no room for a separate one), worked ticket right of centre, Undo / Exit / Primary / Next in a right column that ends above the step chips, and a row of 72 px square cards (its own `CardCompact` template, 6 fit). The middle stays clear for the sandwich. Ghost buttons and the tray tabs now have a dark backing in both layouts (they sit over the 3D view).
- **Roblox's thumbstick and jump button hide while a view holds the character** (`Camera:lockMovement` sets `GuiService.TouchControlsEnabled`): Cook View, CCTV, hiding.
- **Phone geometry without twins:** small moves use `CompactPosition` / `CompactSize` attributes (UIController remembers the regular geometry and swaps); modal panels fill 98% of the safe height on phones (`_fitPanel`, opt out with `NoCompactFit`, change proportions with `CompactPanelSize`). Twins (`XCompact`) are kept for clusters whose contents re-flow (Cook View, Locker grid, Book filters, Shop, van panel).
- **Small visuals keep their size; their Hit grows.** A `TouchMin` constraint on a visible button makes the visual itself 44 px on phones, which covered neighbours (the currency pills' "+" hid the amount; LEAVE VAN spilled out of its panel). Buttons whose look must not grow carry a transparent centred `Hit` child that gets the TouchMin instead (`UIKit.Button`, `UIKit.pillPlus`); controllers wire `UIKit.hit(x)`. Switches next to a label get one invisible hit area over both (Gift, Friends only).
- **Ghost HUD on phones:** Revive moves to the top-left (the shift HUD is hidden for ghosts) and Rattle/Spook rise above the jump button; the Offer card sits top-right above them, with its pitch and price full-width under the close button's touch area.
- **Offer cards use a short `pitch`** (Config.Monetization, ≤ 60 characters) when a product has one; the Shop keeps the full `benefit`. Starter Pack and Coin Boost now have `benefit` lines (they were blank).
- **Labels that hang over the 3D world get a text stroke** (IconButton labels: rail, touch actions, ghost actions) and the hiding hint sits on a dark pill — both were unreadable over bright rooms.
- **Tab pills share one text size** on phones (`tabRows` caps every label at the same size; TextScaled alone sized "Featured" smaller than "Coins").
- **The carried-item label hides while hiding** (LEAVE HIDING sits where it is), as it already did at CCTV.
- **The Supply Locker "+" jumps to Coin packs** (Bible §11.7); it opened the Shop on its default tab.
- **Results rewards show the bonus as Coins** (`bonus = total − coins`); it showed the multiplier.
- **Subtitles move above the Cook View** while cooking (regular: above the step bar; phones: centred on the free middle).
- **Counter bell yields to customers:** prompts share the interact key and Roblox shows only one per key, and the bell sits nearer the staff than the customers do, so the bell prompt is disabled while anyone at the counter has a Serve prompt (it stays reachable through the RingBell remote).
- **Camera never waits for a PlayerModule:** `Camera:_controls()` uses FindFirstChild (newer clients may run without one in PlayerScripts; WaitForChild hung every view that locks movement — the Cook View camera never moved). Movement is locked authoritatively on the server anyway.
- **Maps are generated, not hand-built** (`ServerStorage.BuildTools.ShopMap`, `LobbyMap`, shared `MapKit`): assets placed at exact coordinates or at other assets' sockets, translucent placeholders until an asset is imported, invisible collision + wall segments that also stop nav links crossing walls, and a reachability check of the whole NavNodes graph on every build. Re-run after each import; never hand-edit the map.
- **Shop layout** (80 × 56 + basement): prep line 12 studs from the counter, Break Room door → prep 15, prep → pantry ≈ 20, prep → freezer door ≈ 30, prep → office ≈ 27 (hallway door had to move to the west end because the bin rails line the back wall; Bible says 35), prep → breaker ≈ 70. 6 booths (Bible §4). The counter is a 16-stud pass-through under a header.
- **Doors (Bible §9.6):** `DoorService` + client `Controllers.Doors`. Touch doors (office, restroom, basement, back door) open within 6.5 studs and can be slammed (prompt, noise 30, shut 1.5 s); the front door opens for visitors only and chimes; the Break Room door is one-way during nights (only someone leaving opens it, outsiders get a note). The server only flips attributes; clients swing the leaf with a spring, so it is smooth on every device. The freezer door stays owned by the Shiver module.
- **Collision groups** (`Services.CollisionGroups`): Walls, Ghosts (pass Walls/StaffBarrier), NPCs (pass each other and StaffBarrier), StaffBarrier (front doorway). NPC models are assigned on spawn, including parts added later (looks).
- **Lighting is client-owned** (`Config.Lighting`, `Controllers.Lighting`): mode base (Shift night / Lobby dusk) → room look when the camera is inside `Rooms.Freezer`/`Basement`/`Dining` → dawn from 5:00 AM → blackout grade; the restroom lamp flickers (attribute Flicker). The basement has no light sources (flashlight only, Bible §6.6). Freezer cold mist uses the engine's built-in smoke texture (no external asset).
- **Lobby world** (`LobbyService`, client `LobbyWorld`): `Interact.<Name>` parts get prompts; Kind "Screen" prompts open UI screens client-side (no remote needed). World screens (leaderboards, Employee of the Month, van signs/numbers, daily board, kiosk) are SurfaceGuis built on `Boards.<Name>` parts, Scale-only with TextScaled + size constraints. VIP Lounge barrier is passable for VIP clients; the server returns non-VIPs found inside.
- **StreamingEnabled = false.** Measurement (Studio playtest, client, Lobby with placeholders): content tags Instances 112.5 MB, BaseParts 19.3, GraphicsMeshParts 5.3, GraphicsTexture 55.1, Gui 95.6; Studio's total (3,036 MB) includes the editor, so it is not representative. Each map is ≈ 1,000 parts. Re-measure on a phone (Developer Console) in a live test before launch.
- Default Baseplate and SpawnLocation removed from Workspace (they sat inside the kitchen); spawning is handled by LifeService.
- **Icons travel as NSZ** (base64 of "NSZ1" + u16 w/h + zlib RGBA). Studio's `upload_image` refuses raw.githubusercontent URLs ("Image Url is not trusted"), so the importer plugin (v1.1) inflates NSZ payloads into an EditableImage and publishes them with CreateAssetAsync; IDs land in the generated module `Shared/Generated/Icons`.
- **Sockets:** Blender assets export named gameplay points (`Socket_<Name>` CFrame attributes on the Model) so the map and renderer line up with the models; dimensions are an interface in `blender/STYLE.md`.
- **UIKit lives in `ReplicatedStorage.Client.UIKit`** (Bible says `Client/UIKit`): the edit-time UIBuilder (server context) and client controllers both require it.
- **Touch minimums switch on at runtime:** `TouchMin` UISizeConstraints are stored with MinSize 0 and attribute Min (44 / 72); UIController enables them on touch devices only, so mouse/gamepad layouts are exactly as designed.
- **TV safe area:** on the 10-foot interface UIController maps each interactive screen's top-level clusters into the central 90% (Position 0.05 + 0.9·p, Size 0.9·s) instead of a 16:9-letterboxed root container (a root container with an aspect constraint would stop HUD clusters hugging phone edges).
- **Full-screen layers** (backdrop, FX vignettes, CCTV static, jumpscare, loading/teleport art) are direct children of ScreenInsets.None screens marked `FullScreen = true` and are exempt from the per-child aspect constraint (an aspect constraint would letterbox them).
- **Templates are stored invisible** (GuiObjects under a Folder still render); `UIKit.clone` shows clones.
- **Studio require cache:** edited modules are swapped for fresh clones with `BuildTools.Reload` before re-requiring at edit time.
- **UI standard = AutoScale Lite** (Landon, 2026-10-28). Bible updated to v2.1 (§13.11 + hard rule 11). Supersedes Prompt Phase 7 "created once by its controller … IgnoreGuiInset = true": screens are Studio-authored ScreenGuis in StarterGui, Scale-only, UIAspectRatioConstraint on each top-level child, ScreenInsets = CoreUISafeInsets.
- **Path A uses a local plugin.** `AssetService:CreateAssetAsync` returns "not available yet" from the Studio assistant's execute_luau; Roblox documents it for locally loaded plugins. `studio-plugin/NightShiftImporter.lua` (in this repo, installed to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Roblox\Plugins`) publishes meshes only from this repo's raw URLs; everything else (MeshParts, colours, rigs) is done by `ServerStorage.BuildTools.AssetImport`.
- **Game source code is NOT in this public repo** (anti-cloning); the place file is the source of truth for code.
- **Secret Sandwich icons are public in the repo** (the pipeline serves icons from raw URLs). Recipe text is not stored there (scripts read it from the git-ignored bible copy), but each icon shows its sandwich. This exposes nothing beyond what the uploaded Roblox image assets already do — any image the client can show is fetchable by anyone who finds its ID.
- Config modules added beyond the bible tree: `Config.Player` (movement/stamina/flashlight/life timing) and `Config.Hazards` (power/noise/hiding/CCTV/doors) so numbers stay findable.
- Profile schema additions: `titles`, `title` (level-reward titles need storage).
- Riddle constraints authored per Secret (Config.Riddles); `toast = nil` accepts any state except Burnt.
- Order modifications (Night 3+): 35% chance per human order, one "no X"/"extra X" (bible gives no rate).
- Daily quests drawn deterministically from the UTC day number (same three for everyone).
- `HttpService.HttpEnabled` set true for development (asset/anim fetches). Runtime game code does not use HTTP; turn it off before launch.
- Studio-only `ServerStorage.DevProbe` BindableFunction (created by Bootstrap only in Studio) lets the build session inspect live service state.
